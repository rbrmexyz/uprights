Uprights

Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Uprights doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information. There's no account to make, no tracking and no advertising network in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your best streak, your recent runs and whether sound is on are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best streak is posted to the Uprights leaderboard through Apple. Apple handles your Game Center name and scores under Apple's privacy policy. We only see what any player sees on the leaderboard.

Game settings and sponsor boards

When it opens, the app downloads a small settings file and any sponsor artwork from GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the request (for example your IP address) under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The file contains no information about you, and nothing about you is sent back.

Sponsors

Sponsor logos appear as part of the stadium. They're plain images: they don't track you, and tapping the game never sends you to a sponsor.

Children

Uprights is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions go to the support page.
