How to play

  • Press on the ball and flick up toward the posts. The harder the flick, the further it goes.
  • Strike the middle of the ball. Off to the side, it curves; on top, it skims low; underneath, it balloons.
  • Only kick as hard as the distance needs. Overswinging a short kick makes it wobble.
  • Check the wind in the top right. It pushes the ball most on the way down.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Scores post when a run ends, and only if you're signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). Only your best streak shows.
